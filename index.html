What the data shows

    These figures are the number of deaths, not the rate per person. Countries with larger or older populations tend to have more deaths even when the risk per person is the same, so compare trends within one country rather than between countries. A rising count can also reflect population growth and ageing, or better recording of causes of death, and not only a change in cancer risk. This page describes patterns in the data and does not explain their causes.
